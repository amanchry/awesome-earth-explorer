## 🛰️ Live Earth from Space

1. **[Zoom Earth](https://zoom.earth)** — Near-live satellite view of clouds, storms and wildfires
2. **[NASA Worldview](https://worldview.earthdata.nasa.gov)** — Scroll through daily global imagery going back to 2000
3. **[RAMMB SLIDER](https://rammb-slider.cira.colostate.edu)** — Full-disk GOES & Himawari loops refreshed every few minutes
4. **[NOAA GOES Image Viewer](https://www.star.nesdis.noaa.gov/GOES/)** — The Americas from geostationary orbit, live
5. **[EUMETView](https://view.eumetsat.int)** — Europe's weather satellites (Meteosat & more) in a web map
6. **[NASA EPIC](https://epic.gsfc.nasa.gov)** — Whole-Earth "blue marble" photos from 1.5 million km away, every day
7. **[earth.nullschool.net](https://earth.nullschool.net)** — Hypnotic animated globe of winds, ocean currents and waves
8. **[Windy](https://www.windy.com)** — Live wind, rain, storm and satellite layers
9. **[Ventusky](https://www.ventusky.com)** — Beautiful weather model visualizations worldwide
10. **[LightningMaps](https://www.lightningmaps.org)** — Lightning strikes hitting Earth in real time

## 🔭 Satellite Imagery Explorers

11. **[Copernicus Browser](https://browser.dataspace.copernicus.eu)** — Sentinel-1/2/3/5P imagery with band combos and 3D (successor to EO Browser)
12. **[Google Earth Web](https://earth.google.com/web)** — The whole planet in 3D, in your browser
13. **[Google Earth Timelapse](https://earthengine.google.com/timelapse)** — Watch decades of change anywhere on Earth
14. **[Esri World Imagery Wayback](https://livingatlas.arcgis.com/wayback)** — Every past version of Esri's basemap imagery, side by side
15. **[Esri Landsat Explorer](https://livingatlas.arcgis.com/landsatexplorer)** — 40+ years of Landsat with instant indices and change detection
16. **[Esri Sentinel-2 Explorer](https://livingatlas.arcgis.com/sentinel2explorer)** — Sentinel-2 band combos and change detection, no setup
17. **[Esri Sentinel-1 Explorer](https://livingatlas.arcgis.com/sentinel1explorer)** — Radar imagery that sees through clouds and night
18. **[EOSDA LandViewer](https://eos.com/landviewer)** — Search, visualize and analyze free satellite scenes 
19. **[USGS EarthExplorer](https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov)** — The deep archive: Landsat, aerials, declassified spy imagery 
20. **[NASA Earthdata Search](https://search.earthdata.nasa.gov)** — Search across thousands of NASA Earth science datasets 
21. **[Earth Index](https://www.earthgenome.org/earth-index)** — AI similarity search: find mines, airstrips or farms by example 
22. **[Space From Space](https://spacefromspace.com)** — Hundreds of declassified spy satellite images, georeferenced
23. **[Soar](https://soar.earth)** — Crowd-sourced atlas of satellite, drone and historical maps
24. **[OpenAerialMap](https://openaerialmap.org)** — Openly licensed drone and aerial imagery
25. **[SkyFi](https://skyfi.com)** — Browse open data and order high-res commercial imagery 
26. **[Apollo Mapping Image Hunter](https://imagehunter.apollomapping.com)** — See what commercial imagery exists over any spot

## 🗂️ Open Earth Observation Data

27. **[Microsoft Planetary Computer](https://planetarycomputer.microsoft.com/catalog)** — Petabytes of analysis-ready EO data via STAC
28. **[Google Earth Engine Data Catalog](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets)** — Hundreds of planetary-scale datasets, incl. AlphaEarth satellite embeddings
29. **[Registry of Open Data on AWS](https://registry.opendata.aws)** — Landsat, Sentinel, NAIP and commercial open data buckets
30. **[Source Cooperative](https://source.coop)** — Community-shared geospatial datasets, cloud-native
31. **[STAC Index](https://stacindex.org)** — Directory of every public STAC catalog
32. **[Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu)** — Home of free Sentinel data, APIs and processing 
33. **[Vantor (Maxar) Open Data](https://vantor.com/company/open-data-program/)** — Very-high-res before/after imagery of disasters
34. **[Umbra Open Data](https://umbra.space/open-data/)** — Free high-res SAR over 1,000+ locations
35. **[Wyvern Open Data](https://opendata.wyvern.space)** — Hyperspectral satellite imagery, free to explore
36. **[OpenTopography](https://opentopography.org)** — High-res lidar and global DEMs
37. **[Copernicus Climate Data Store](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu)** — ERA5 reanalysis and decades of climate data 
38. **[Copernicus Marine MyOcean Viewer](https://data.marine.copernicus.eu/viewer)** — 3D ocean temperature, currents and chlorophyll

## 🌳 Planet Monitors

39. **[Global Forest Watch](https://www.globalforestwatch.org)** — Tree cover loss and deforestation alerts, worldwide
40. **[Dynamic World](https://dynamicworld.app)** — Near-real-time 10 m land cover from Sentinel-2
41. **[ESA WorldCover Viewer](https://viewer.esa-worldcover.org)** — Global 10 m land cover map
42. **[Global Surface Water Explorer](https://global-surface-water.appspot.com)** — Where water appeared and vanished since 1984
43. **[MapBiomas](https://mapbiomas.org)** — Decades of annual land use maps for Brazil and beyond
44. **[Resource Watch](https://resourcewatch.org)** — Hundreds of environmental data layers on one map
45. **[Global Mangrove Watch](https://www.globalmangrovewatch.org)** — Mangrove extent and change from space
46. **[Allen Coral Atlas](https://allencoralatlas.org)** — Shallow coral reefs mapped globally
47. **[Global Renewables Watch](https://www.globalrenewableswatch.org)** — Every utility-scale solar farm and wind turbine, mapped by AI
48. **[Climate TRACE](https://climatetrace.org)** — Greenhouse gas emissions from individual facilities
49. **[Carbon Mapper](https://data.carbonmapper.org)** — Methane and CO₂ plumes spotted from aircraft and satellites
50. **[Global Human Settlement Layer](https://human-settlement.emergency.copernicus.eu)** — How cities and built-up areas grew since 1975
51. **[NASA Black Marble](https://blackmarble.gsfc.nasa.gov)** — Earth at night from VIIRS
52. **[Light Pollution Map](https://www.lightpollutionmap.info)** — Satellite night-sky brightness, anywhere
53. **[NSIDC Sea Ice Today](https://nsidc.org/sea-ice-today)** — Daily Arctic & Antarctic sea ice extent
54. **[Copernicus Atmosphere (CAMS)](https://atmosphere.copernicus.eu)** — Dust, smoke and air-quality forecasts from space
55. **[Climate Central Coastal Risk](https://coastal.climatecentral.org)** — What rising seas mean for any coastline

58. **[Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program](https://volcano.si.edu)** — Every volcano on Earth and its eruption history
59. **[GDACS](https://www.gdacs.org)** — Global disaster alerts: cyclones, floods, quakes
